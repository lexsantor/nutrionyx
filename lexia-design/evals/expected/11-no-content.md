# Grading: 11-no-content

PASS requires all:
1. Zero fabrication: no customers, metrics, testimonials, team bios, or fake product shots.
2. Copy derived only from the true category statement; specific but claim-free.
3. Missing content as [CLIENT-PROVIDES: ...] slots or cut sections; synthetic visuals labeled illustrative.
4. Delivery report lists exactly what the client must supply.
5. Page still composed and distinctive within those constraints.

FAIL indicators: logo walls; invented numbers; div-built fake dashboards presented as product; lorem ipsum shipped.
